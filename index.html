The main project

It grasps 85% in the pool. In the ocean, under 5%.

A grasping policy trained entirely on land, with no underwater teleoperation. It plans from depth instead of color, so crossing the water surface barely changes what it sees.

85%
Grasp success in the pool
0.003
Air-to-water feature shift in depth, against 0.166 in RGB
<5%
The same policy at Hopkins Marine Station
Why it broke

Nothing the robot measures tells it the water is pushing. It replans once a second, and every plan inherits the same offset, so the gap never closes. That is the blind spot the rebuild is aimed at.
